## Partial / still hard at municipal scale

| GPC | Gap |
|---|---|
| I.1 / I.2 | Homogeneous **open** electricity & gas extracts by **city polygon × customer class** (Commerce filings are utility/county; Xcel/city NDAs still common) |

## Recommended next acquisition targets

1. Standardize **city-boundary utility class extracts** (electric + gas) via Commerce/PUC open template or Xcel/muni data-sharing MOUs.
2. Publish **community-generated VMT** (or document through-traffic adjustment) for all municipalities using MnDOT AADT.
3. Open **multi-year CIRIS/GPC tables** for Minneapolis & Saint Paul (not only PDF/dashboard summaries).
4. Extend Met Council methods to a **municipal pack** for non-metro Minnesota cities (county → place downscale recipe).
