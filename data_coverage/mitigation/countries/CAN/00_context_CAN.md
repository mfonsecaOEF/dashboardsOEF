## Structural notes

1. Canada is **data-rich at national/provincial and facility scales**; many GPC needs are directly met or closely proxied.
2. **City gaps** remain utility customer-class extracts, municipal VKT, and refrigerant stocks — but strong city examples exist (Toronto, Calgary, BC CEEI).
3. Prefer **NIR/GHGRP/StatCan** over modeled products when both exist.
4. Only residual category **I.6** lacked a dedicated mapped dataset in this pass (gap row added).
