## Partial / dated but usable with caution

- Addis Ababa GPC inventory **2016** (needs update)
- Vehicle fleet figures often **~2014–2015** in public compilations
- Waste figures mostly from **project briefs**, not annual open APIs
- Aviation from **carrier reports**, not airport authority open stats

## Recommended next acquisition targets

1. EEU anonymized electricity sales by woreda and customer class for Addis + secondary cities.
2. FTA current vehicle registry extract by region/city and fuel type.
3. AACMA / municipal annual disposed tonnage & composition for priority cities.
4. AAWSA WWTP influent loads and population served by catchment.
5. MoWE/petroleum distributor city-level fuel sales (diesel, gasoline, jet, LPG).
