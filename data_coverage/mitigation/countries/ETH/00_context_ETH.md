## Structural notes for city inventories

1. **AFOLU dominates national emissions** (BUR1); city inventories still need strong stationary energy + transport + waste for urban BASIC.
2. **Residential energy is biomass-heavy** outside/alongside grid electricity — MTF/HCES/LSMS are essential supporting sources.
3. **Addis Ababa has a published GPC inventory (2016)** — unusual positive; other cities generally lack open GPC products.
4. **EEU retail electricity by woreda/customer class** is operationally critical but was **not found as a systematic open download**.
5. **Waterborne navigation** inside Ethiopia is limited to lakes/river craft; international maritime activity occurs mainly via Djibouti (outside national boundary accounting choices).
