## Notable positives vs Morocco

- Published **city GPC inventory** for Addis Ababa (2016).
- Very strong **annual agricultural sample survey** stack for livestock/crops.
- Mature **forest MRV / FREL** programme.

## Notable weaknesses vs Morocco

- Weaker open **vehicle registration** and **airport authority** statistical portals.
- No domestic **seaport** statistics (landlocked).
- Retail electricity openness lower (no Lydec-like public volume filings found).
