### I.8 Fugitive oil & gas — **None / very limited**

## Cross-cutting verdict

Ethiopia’s **strongest open layers are AFOLU (AgSS + forest MRV), national electricity generation (EEP/MoWE), and the Addis Ababa 2016 GPC inventory**. City-scale gaps mirror many low/middle-income contexts: **no systematic open retail electricity by customer class**, weak **VKT/fuel sales**, and **waste facility time series**. Biomass for residential energy is first-order and better captured via **surveys (MTF/HCES/LSMS)** than utility data alone.
