## Structural notes

1. Australia is **strong at national/state and large-facility scales** (NGA, AES, NGER).
2. Unlike the UK, there is **no official government LGA GHG National Statistic** for every council — **Snapshot Climate** is the main nationwide municipal product (GPC-oriented, not official).
3. Prefer **NGA / NGER / AES / BITRE** over Snapshot or international modeled products when both exist; use Snapshot as LGA starting point.
4. Only residual category **I.6** lacked a dedicated mapped dataset in this pass (gap row added).
