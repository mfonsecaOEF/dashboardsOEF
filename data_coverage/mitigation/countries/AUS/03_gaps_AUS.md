## Recommended next acquisition targets

1. National or AER-coordinated LGA electricity/gas consumption extracts by customer class.
2. State open VKT / odometer programs beyond capital-city BITRE tables.
3. More councils publishing open tabular GPC inventories (Melbourne model).
4. Refrigerant stock / F-gas microdata suitable for LGA IPPU.
