- `related_gpc_sector` (singular)
- `row_id` (e.g. `AUS-001-I.1`)
- `NO-COVERAGE-{sector}` for uncovered sectors
- Sorted by GPC code

## Comparative note

Among countries in this batch, Australia sits between **Canada** (strong facilities + city examples) and the **UK** (official LA GHG for every authority). Australia’s differentiator is **NGER/Safeguard facility reporting** plus **Snapshot** as a nationwide LGA GPC-style product without an official LA National Statistic.
