## Cross-cutting verdict

Australia has **excellent official coverage at national/state and facility scales**, plus **BITRE capital-city VKT** and a **nationwide LGA product (Snapshot)**. Compared with the UK, the weak spot is the absence of an official DESNZ-style LA GHG National Statistic and uneven open DNSP meter-by-LGA data. Mapped gap: **I.6** only.
