## Partial but actionable with LGU outreach

- Distribution utility electricity by sector **inside LGU boundary** (Meralco/ECs — request process documented)
- LTO provincial vehicle extracts (often on request/FOI)
- LGU 10-year Solid Waste Management Plans (facility tonnage)

## Recommended next acquisition targets

1. Formal data-sharing MOUs with Meralco and major Visayas/Mindanao DUs for LGU×tariff sales.
2. LTO open release of city/municipal registration by fuel type (beyond FOI).
3. EMB national sanitary landfill annual tonnage/composition dashboard.
4. DOTr published VKT or odometer-based fleet activity.
5. DENR/DTI refrigerant import & stock statistics for IPPU product use.
