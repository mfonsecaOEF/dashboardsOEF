## Structural notes for city inventories

1. **Metered electricity at city scale exists operationally** (distributors/SRMs) but is only **partially public** (e.g. Lydec filings for Casablanca volumes). Systematic open municipal customer-class extracts were not found.
2. **National inventory (NIR)** is strong for emissions totals and sector methods, but **not city-boundary**.
3. **Transport activity** is relatively open for fleet (NARSA), airports (ONDA), ports (ANP/TMPA); **VKT and municipal fuel sales** remain weak.
4. **Waste** is policy-rich (PNDM) but **facility-year tonnage/composition open data** is thin.
5. **Modeled international products** (EDGAR, Climate TRACE, GFW, WorldCover) are important gap-fills for city spatialization.
