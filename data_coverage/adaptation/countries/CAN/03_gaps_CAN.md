# Gaps — Canada (CAN) CCRA / adaptation

## Hard gap

| ID | Indicator | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| CCRA-039 | Urban stormwater drainage coverage | No consistent open Canada-wide municipal drainage GIS; city engineering layers usually internal |

## Soft gaps

| Theme | Issue |
|---|---|
| Disease cases | CCHS/PHAC aggregates — not open case GIS |
| Regulatory flood maps | CFM shows where maps exist; access/license patchy by province |
| Landslide national COG | Weaker than flood FS; regional inventories |
| Utility electricity microdata | LDC customer-class rarely open (same as GPC) |
| Water access indicator | Less discriminating in urban Canada (near-universal piped water) |
| Great Lakes levels | Distinct from marine CanCoast SLR — needs lake-specific products for inland coasts |

## Relative strength

With PHL, **CAN is among the strongest** adaptation packages: DA E/V + national flood susceptibility + coastal SLR tools make city CCRA relatively straightforward, especially given OEF’s existing MN pipeline.
