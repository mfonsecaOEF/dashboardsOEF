# Gaps — Australia (AUS) CCRA / adaptation

## Hard gap

| ID | Indicator | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| CCRA-039 | Urban stormwater drainage coverage | No open national (or systematic LGA) pipe/network coverage inventory; council drainage GIS usually restricted |

**Related (not a substitute):** state/AFRIP flood studies, DEA WOfS inundation frequency, OEF `drainage_constrained` (`AUS-A037`).

## Soft gaps

| Theme | Issue |
|---|---|
| Disease cases | AIHW/state surveillance not open as city-block GIS; use facility density + Census/SEIFA health proxies |
| Flood seamlessness | AFRIP + state portals are federated — not one national AEP layer (same pattern as Canada CFM) |
| Energy poverty | No DESNZ-style fuel poverty SA1 product; construct from Census + SEIFA + AES |
| Landslide | National susceptibility not seamless; inventory density uneven |
| Buildings | Geoscape often commercial — use OSM/Microsoft for open CCRA |
| Water/sanitation | Near-universal urban piped services — low discrimination at SA1 |

## Relative strength

With **PHL / GBR / CAN**, **AUS is top-tier** for city CCRA: SA1+SEIFA neighbourhood V, solid climate (BOM/CCiA), and workable flood via AFRIP/state + WOfS. Weaker than GBR on a single national flood+CC product and on dedicated fuel poverty.
